How to Play
SWIPE TO SLIDE: Swipe or use WASD / Arrows to slide. Worms slide until hitting a wall.
FILL WITHIN LIMITS: Grow each worm to fill all tiles within its target length limit (top HUD).
SWITCH WORMS: Tap the other worm or press SPACE to switch active control!
HINTS & RESTART: Use HINT if you get stuck, or tap ↺ to restart anytime.
EARN REWARDS: Clear stages to earn Wreaths & unlock fun new skins in the shop!
